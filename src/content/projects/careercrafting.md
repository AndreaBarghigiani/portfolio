CareerCrafting helps software engineers keep a structured record of their work and growth. Its evidence can support performance reviews, promotion cases, resumes, and interview preparation.

I built the product around deliberate capture: an engineer chooses when to save useful work through an AI agent or MCP connection. The resulting memories are organized for later reflection and career conversations.
