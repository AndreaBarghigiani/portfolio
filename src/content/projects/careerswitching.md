CareerSwitching supports the practical work of a job search: collecting opportunities, ranking them, managing applications, preparing tailored materials, and keeping track of interviews, follow-ups, and outcomes. It complements CareerCrafting, which keeps the evidence behind a person’s experience.

I’m building it as a focused workspace that keeps the moving parts of a search connected, so candidates can make considered decisions and follow each opportunity through.
